A FAMILY BUSINESS PROJECT
THE PROJECT

A stronger digital
front door.

The challenge was to help a local clinic present itself clearly online and make it easier for nearby people to find useful business information.

This was a family-business project where I contributed to the clinic's digital presence. The work combined visibility, information and communication across several online and offline touchpoints.

BUSINESSSkin Care Clinic
LOCATIONSeoni, Madhya Pradesh
MY CONTRIBUTIONDigital presence & marketing
FOCUSLocal discovery
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Good local service needs clear visibility.

The clinic needed a more coherent online presence so people could discover its location and services, understand the business and find a way to make contact. Its digital touchpoints needed attention, from business information to website presentation.

02 / THE WORK

Bring the important touchpoints together.

I worked on the Google Business Profile, created a website, developed promotional pamphlets and banners, and produced YouTube videos and other digital marketing material. The goal was to make the clinic's information easier to find and its presentation more consistent.

04 / WHAT I LEARNED

Digital work is connected work.

A website, a business profile and promotional content are more useful when they tell a consistent story. The project reinforced the value of clear information, local relevance and making the next step obvious for a potential customer.
