01 — MY STORY

how things work.

I'm Ahmed, a full-stack developer, SEO expert and digital marketing specialist from Seoni, Madhya Pradesh.

My path has moved across science, commerce, technology and business. Along the way, I became increasingly interested in how digital tools can solve practical problems for real businesses.

That interest turned into hands-on work: building websites, improving local search visibility, creating marketing material and experimenting with AI-powered products. I enjoy the mix of logic and creativity involved in taking an idea from a rough outline to something people can actually use.

I am currently pursuing B.Com while continuing to develop my technical and business skills.
